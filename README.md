## What Best Buy Cashback Actually Is (and Why It's Not a Coupon)

Cashback is not a discount. A discount lowers the number you see at checkout. Cashback is money that lands in a separate account — your ShopBack wallet — after Best Buy confirms the transaction, and you withdraw it later to PayPal or a bank account. The two layers are funded by different budgets inside Best Buy, which is exactly why they stack.

Here's the simple version of how it works:

1. You click through a cashback portal (like ShopBack) to Best Buy.
2. You shop and check out exactly as you normally would.
3. The portal earns an affiliate commission from Best Buy for sending you.
4. The portal passes most of that commission back to you as cashback.
5. After Best Buy confirms the order (and the return window closes), the cashback moves from "tracked" to "confirmed" and becomes withdrawable.

The key insight: cashback is calculated on the price you actually pay, after any sale or coupon. So a lower checkout total means a lower cashback dollar amount, but the percentage stays the same. You're not "choosing" between a deal and cashback — you get both.

## The Four-Layer Stacking Strategy: How to Actually Pay Less

| Layer | Funded By | Stacks With | What Breaks It |

| Sale price (Deal of the Day, Black Friday, etc.) | Best Buy's pricing | All other layers | Nothing — it's the base |
| Promo code | Best Buy's marketing budget | All other layers, if sourced safely | Sourcing the code from a third-party coupon site overwrites the affiliate cookie |

**The correct stacking order:** sale price → merchant-approved promo code → ShopBack cashback → rewards credit card.

## Common Questions About Best Buy Cashback

**Does Best Buy have its own cashback program?** Sort of. Best Buy's My Best Buy loyalty program earns points on every purchase (1% back for Plus/Total members, up to 6% back with the My Best Buy Credit Card on qualifying purchases). Those points convert to Best Buy Bucks you can spend at Best Buy. It's a closed-loop reward, not withdrawable cash. ShopBack cashback is real money you can withdraw to PayPal. The two stack — you earn Best Buy points *and* ShopBack cashback on the same order.

**Can I use a Best Buy coupon and still earn ShopBack cashback?** Yes, as long as the coupon is listed on ShopBack's Best Buy merchant page or comes from Best Buy's own newsletter/app. Coupons from third-party aggregator sites can overwrite the tracking cookie and void your cashback.

**What if my cashback doesn't track?** Wait 2–7 days. If it still doesn't appear, submit a missing-cashback claim from your ShopBack account with your order number and purchase date. ShopBack's support team will follow up with Best Buy's affiliate network to recover it.

**Is ShopBack safe for Best Buy purchases?** Yes. ShopBack routes the click to Best Buy via a standard affiliate link. The purchase itself happens on Best Buy's own checkout, under Best Buy's payment, delivery, and return policies. ShopBack never sees your card details.

**Does cashback work on Best Buy Open-Box items?** Generally no — refurbished and Open-Box items are on the exclusion list. Always verify on the merchant page before assuming.

**Can I stack Best Buy Trade-In with cashback?** Yes. The trade-in credit reduces the price at checkout, and ShopBack cashback tracks on the net paid amount for eligible categories. The two layers don't conflict.

**What's the single biggest mistake people make?** Grabbing a promo code from a coupon-aggregator site right before checkout. That one click silently overwrites the affiliate cookie and voids the cashback. Use codes from ShopBack's merchant page or Best Buy's own channels, and the chain stays intact.
